Privacy Policy

Hermes Gmail Readonly is a private personal-use application. It accesses Gmail only after explicit Google OAuth authorization by the account owner.

The application requests only the Gmail read-only scope. It may read message metadata and content solely to identify important email and prepare personal summaries.

The application does not send, delete, modify, archive, label, forward, or reply to email.

OAuth credentials and tokens are not intentionally published on this website. This website does not collect Gmail content, passwords, OAuth tokens, or other account secrets.

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security permissions page.
